Research date: 2026-11-14. Keep Roomline's existing forest, paper, and charcoal identity. References inform hierarchy and interaction, not competitor claims or copied branding.

## Commitments

- Public marketing and account-entry screens keep a scoped light palette even when a workspace uses dark mode. The operational app and component reference support light/dark themes.
- Forest primary actions, charcoal body text, warm paper marketing canvas, white operational surfaces; semantic success/warning/error never interchangeable with branding.
- Display 48–72px desktop/36–44px mobile; body 16px minimum marketing; real operational controls 14–16px. Tiny screenshot-like preview labels must be replaced with readable controls or noninteractive imagery.
- 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96px spacing; 8px controls, 12px panels; limited shadows on raised layers.
- At least 44px interactive targets on touch; visible labels, focus indicators, semantic controls, safe areas, reduced-motion parity.
- Reuse installed Radix/shadcn primitives and existing contracts; do not bulk-reinstall a React19/Tailwind4 registry over this React18/Tailwind3 app.
- Marketing pages explain actual capabilities and deployment dependencies. No invented prices, client logos, compliance certification, urgency, analytics, or '100x better' claims.

## Source-status limits

Refero styles and bundled DESIGN.md studies are reference material, not a live app audit. Competitor screenshots, local screenshot evidence, and source-only findings are labeled separately in the assessment. Missing native app source cannot be represented as a native revamp.
