How Ideals VDR works and when to use it
What is a virtual data room?
A
data room is
a secure online repository for storing and sharing confidential information during complex business transactions such as mergers and acquisitions. The
Ideals platform
gives your deal team a dedicated workspace to manage this process efficiently and securely.
How can I use Ideals?
The Ideals platform runs securely in your web browser, and you can also install our mobile app for an optimal “on-the-go” experience. Once your data room is set up, you can upload documents, manage user access, and collaborate with your deal team in one protected workspace.
Start your free trial now
to see Ideals in action.
What are the types of virtual data rooms?
Virtual data room solutions generally fall into three categories: established providers such as Intralinks, modern platforms like Ideals, and general-purpose
file-sharing tools
such as Dropbox, which lack the granular permissions, audit trails, Q&A, and deal-specific controls of a
dedicated M&A tool
.
What are the main benefits of a data room?
Virtual data rooms provide a secure environment for sharing sensitive corporate data, with granular access controls to protect confidential information. They also maintain full audit trails for accountability and built-in collaboration tools that keep all parties aligned throughout a transaction.
What data room features does Ideals offer?
Ideals’ virtual data room includes deal-focused features such as granular permissions, due diligence checklists, reporting, Q&A, document versioning, external link sharing, and multi-project management, depending on the selected plan.
See the
overview of VDR features
for the complete picture.
What types of use cases benefit most from a data room?
An
online data room
is the right choice for securely sharing sensitive information with multiple parties. M&A transactions, fundraising, and real estate deals all involve confidential documents that can be vulnerable to data breaches. Ideals’
dataroom services
are purpose-built for these scenarios, combining the security required to protect confidential data with the collaboration tools that keep time-critical deals moving.
What file types can I store in an Ideals data room?
Ideals VDR
supports the most common formats — Word and PDF documents for legal and financial records, Excel and CSV files for financial data, PowerPoint for presentations, and image formats for visual assets. Ideals also supports video files. See the
full list of supported file types
for details.
Do I need training to set up and use an Ideals data room?
Ideals is designed for non-technical users and can be set up in minutes. For teams that want additional guidance, Ideals offers live training with a dedicated expert to get up to speed quickly, as well as data room setup assistance, Premier project support, and Premier Professional Services for project-specific needs.
How can I prepare a data room for due diligence or an M&A deal?
Preparing a
secure data room
for business transactions involves a few steps: creating the data room structure, uploading and organizing the relevant documents, configuring secure access permissions by role, and onboarding participants.
Our
due diligence checklist
for sell-side VDR users outlines which documents you should include in the data room during the due diligence process.
How we protect your data
How secure is the Ideals data room compared to traditional file-sharing platforms?
Unlike email or generic file-sharing tools, Ideals VDR uses
enterprise-grade encryption
, multi-factor authentication, and granular access controls. This helps keep your data room protected throughout the deal.
Does Ideals provide detailed audit trails for compliance and legal purposes?
Yes. Our virtual data room generates comprehensive audit logs that capture key user actions, including views, downloads, and permission changes. This helps protect your data and supports compliance, auditability, and accountability.
What types of encryption does Ideals use to protect confidential documents?
Ideals VDR uses AES-256 encryption, a gold standard in data security. That means even in the unlikely event that unauthorized personnel intercept your files, they’ll still need the decryption key to access or read them.
What measures are in place to prevent unauthorized data leaks during due diligence?
Features such as dynamic watermarking, fence view, and IP restrictions help reduce the risk of unauthorized access, copying, screenshots, and data leaks, while activity dashboards and customizable alerts instantly notify if they detect unusual behavior in your data room.
What happens to our data if we terminate the contract? Do we control deletion and archiving?
Yes. You control data deletion in your virtual data room. You can also create a USB archive or download an offline backup to keep your project documents safe.
How to compare your options
How should I choose data room software?
The most important criteria for evaluating an
online data room
are security features, security certifications, independent security ratings, deal-specific features, pricing transparency, and the quality and availability of customer support.
We also recommend testing a shortlist through free trials for a hands-on virtual data room comparison.
How does Ideals VDR compare to other data room providers?
As a
virtual data room
, Ideals combines an intuitive interface with advanced security features and collaboration tools, including eight granular permission levels, dynamic watermarks, advanced Q&A workflows, and detailed audit logs. These are backed by transparent usage-based pricing, independent security certifications, and 24/7 multilingual support. See the full
VDR comparison and selection guide
.
When to use alternatives to virtual data rooms?
Virtual data room software
may be overkill for routine internal document storage, everyday team collaboration, or basic file distribution with no requirements for secure document sharing. In those cases, standard cloud storage tools are a more practical fit. A VDR is the right choice when sensitive data and controlled access are essential.
When are free data rooms an option?
Free data rooms or free file-sharing tools may work for non-critical storage and basic sharing, but they are usually not suitable for complex transactions or confidential data shared with third parties. However, most reputable providers, including Ideals, offer a free trial — Ideals’ being fully functional — useful for evaluating the platform before committing to a paid plan.
How pricing models and plans work
What is the Ideals VDR pricing structure?
Ideals uses a transparent usage-based pricing model — you pay for the storage you actually use, with no hidden fees or ‘per-page’ charges. This makes budgeting straightforward and predictable. For a broader overview, see the factors affecting
virtual data room pricing
.
What pricing plans does Ideals offer?
Ideals offers three plans:
Core
for small to medium deals with standard features,
Premier
for larger transactions requiring extended services and dedicated support, and
Enterprise
for high-volume deal pipelines requiring multi-project management and API integrations. See all plans at
Ideals VDR pricing
.
What are some other data room pricing models?
Some
VDR providers
charge ‘per-page’ — an outdated model with
well-documented pitfalls
, including unpredictable document management costs and penalty fees for extensions. Per-user and flat-fee models require upfront estimation and may charge for unused storage. Ideals’ transparent usage-based pricing helps reduce these risks by aligning costs with storage and project needs.
Where can I find a pricing calculator for an Ideals VDR?
Ideals’ pricing is storage-based with a single rate for all file types and no hidden charges. Rather than a self-serve calculator, Ideals offers a direct cost estimate tailored to your project size, security requirements, service needs, and storage needs.
